# 5 Missed-Call Recovery Scripts (Free)
**Vaexor** — use today. No signup.

These are SMS / voicemail / callback openers for any small business that loses jobs when the phone rings out.

## 1. Same-day SMS (plumber / HVAC / trades)
> Hey {{name}} — sorry we missed your call. Can still get you on today’s route if you reply with address + what broke. Prefer a callback time?

## 2. After-hours voicemail
> Thanks for calling {{biz}}. We’re closed now, but leave your name, number, and issue — we’ll text a window first thing. For emergencies reply URGENT.

## 3. No-show follow-up
> We saw a missed call and couldn’t reach you back. Still need help with {{job}}? Reply YES and we’ll lock a slot.

## 4. Quote-request soft close
> Got your call about {{job}}. Ballpark for similar jobs has been {{range}}. Want me to hold Thursday 10–12 or Friday 2–4?

## 5. Review ask after win
> Glad we fixed {{job}}. If we earned it, a 30-second Google review helps more than ads: {{review_link}}
