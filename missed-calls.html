Missed calls are unpaid invoices.

Restaurants, shops, offices, clinics, contractors — when you’re busy, the phone still rings. Callers who don’t get an answer usually don’t leave a voicemail. They call the next place.

Lost-revenue sketch

3 missed calls this week ≈ $450–$900 walking out.

If even one of those callers would have booked a typical small-ticket job, order, or appointment, that’s real money — every quiet week, not just peak season.

Assumptions (labeled, not a case study): average booked ticket ~$150–$300 (lunch catering, retail special order, service visit, consult). Conversion if someone answered: ~1 in 3 missed calls. Your numbers will differ — plug in your own average ticket and miss rate.

What the agent does

An AI front desk that covers the gap when you can’t pick up — for any business that lives on the phone and inbox.

Missed-call catch

When a call goes unanswered, the agent can text or call back fast: who you are, that you got the message, and the next useful question.

Email intake

Reads basic inquiry emails, pulls name / need / timing, and drafts a clear reply you can send or auto-send with your rules.

Scheduling & handoff

Collects availability, books into a simple calendar slot (or queues for you), and leaves a clean note instead of a voicemail graveyard.
